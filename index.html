STUDIO METHODOLOGY

HOW IT WORKS

From first visit to final handover, you know what happens each day.

01 Day 1 – 2

Site Visit

We visit your home, measure every wall and check plumbing and electrical points.

02 Day 3 – 5

3D Design

We make a 3D design of your room. You choose shutter colours, handles and drawer layouts.

03 Day 6 – 7

Fixed Quote

You get an itemised quote with board, finish and hardware details. No hidden extras.

04 Day 21 – 30

Build & Fit

Cabinets are cut and edge-banded in our factory, then fitted at your home within 21 to 30 days.
